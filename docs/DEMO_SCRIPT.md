# Pathly — 2–3 minute jury demo

Goal: show one connected path and prove the personalisation is real, not hardcoded.
Everything below happens live; the only thing prepared in advance is the demo button.

## Before you start

- Open the deployed URL in a **phone-sized window** (or on a real phone).
- Clear state so you start clean: the "Reset" is not needed if you use a fresh browser profile.
- Have the Data & sources screen one tap away.

## The script

**0:00 — Landing.**
One sentence: "Pathly turns a student's goals into a scored admission plan — no chat, no catalog."
Point at the four-step strip: Profile → Match → Compare → Plan.

**0:15 — Profile.**
Tap **Try a demo dossier** to load Aliya (Grade 11, Kazakhstan, Computer Science, budget $6k,
IELTS 6.0). Say: "This is eight inputs — change any of them and the whole plan recomputes."

**0:35 — Diagnosis.**
Open the Diagnosis step, answer two or three questions out loud, and let the readiness panel
appear: strengths, gaps, advisory discipline. Emphasise: "This is deterministic, not a model."

**0:55 — Recommendations.**
Back to Matches. Three or more programs with a **fit score** and a factor breakdown.
Open one card's **Why this fits** — note the badge that says whether the text came from the AI
or from the template. "If the model is down, we still explain every match."

**1:25 — The live change (the key moment).**
In the **Live sensitivity** panel, add **US** to destinations and set the budget to **$30k**.
Arizona State University jumps to the top and is tagged **NEW ENTRY**; the others show **▼ 1**.
Say: "Same code, new input, different result — that is the personalisation."

**1:50 — Comparison.**
Add two programs to compare, open **Compare**: cost, living cost, total per year, exam
requirements, GPA, teaching language, deadlines, main constraint — side by side.

**2:15 — Roadmap + progress.**
Open **Roadmap**: five phases (exams, documents, deadlines, academic, activities), 14 steps
built from the shortlist and the profile gaps. Show **Your next action**, tap **Mark as done**,
watch the percentage move and the next action switch to the next deadline.

**2:40 — Sources.**
Open **Data & sources**: "Deadlines marked *demo data* are demonstrative and link to the
official admissions pages. We never present an unverified number as a fact."

## If something goes wrong

- **AI is slow or fails** → nothing breaks; explanations silently become template text and the
  badge changes from "AI" to "template". Say it out loud — it is a strength, not a bug.
- **No internet** → the whole path except AI explanations still works.
- **A click misbehaves** → stay on the roadmap, tick a step, and narrate the result.

## What NOT to do

- Do not read the screens word by word.
- Do not claim admission probabilities — Pathly deliberately does not produce them.
- Do not skip the live change; it is the single most convincing 20 seconds of the demo.
